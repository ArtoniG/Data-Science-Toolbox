# Welcome to DS Toolbox

The `ds_toolbox` is designed for Data Scientists working in advanced analytics and modelling teams. It bridges the gap between Scikit-Learn's flexibility and strict regulatory requirements.

## Architecture Map
- [Metrics Layer](api/metrics.md): Gini, KS, IV, and PSI functions.
- [Preprocessing Layer](api/preprocessing.md): WOE and imputation modules.
- [Governance Layer](api/governance.md): Pipeline auditors and reason codes.
